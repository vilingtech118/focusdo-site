Who we are

FocusDo is an iPhone app published by VilingTech (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the FocusDo app and its widget.

What we collect

Nothing. FocusDo does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. We do not operate any server that receives your information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

Where your tasks are stored

  • On your iPhone. Your tasks, due dates, reminders, notes and focus-timer settings are stored locally on your device.
  • In your private iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, FocusDo syncs your tasks between your devices using Apple’s CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. This data belongs to your Apple Account; we have no access to it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • In the widget and Live Activity. The Home Screen widget and the focus timer on your Lock Screen read your tasks from a container shared on your own device. Nothing leaves your iPhone for this.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, FocusDo schedules reminders and focus-timer alerts locally on your device. They are not sent through any server. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › FocusDo.

Backups you create

You can export your tasks as a JSON file and restore from one. The file is saved wherever you choose (for example Files or iCloud Drive). We never receive it.

Your choices and rights

  • Export your data at any time from Settings › Backup in the app.
  • Delete your data by deleting your tasks in the app, or by deleting the app. To remove the synced copy, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › FocusDo.
  • Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Children

FocusDo is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a future version of FocusDo ever needed to collect data, we would ask for your consent first.

我们是谁

FocusDo 是由 VilingTech（下称"我们"）发布的 iPhone 应用。本政策说明你在使用 FocusDo 应用及其小组件时，你的信息会如何被处理。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。FocusDo 不收集、不存储、不出售、也不分享任何个人数据。我们没有运营任何会接收你信息的服务器；应用中不包含任何数据分析、广告或追踪代码，也没有任何第三方 SDK。

你的任务存在哪里

  • 在你的 iPhone 上。你的任务、截止日期、提醒、备注和专注计时设置都保存在你的设备本地。
  • 在你的私有 iCloud 里。如果你登录了 iCloud，FocusDo 会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit，在你的私有 iCloud 数据库中同步你在各设备上的任务。这些数据属于你的 Apple 账户，我们无法访问。Apple 如何处理 iCloud 数据，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。
  • 在小组件和实时活动中。主屏幕小组件和锁屏上的专注计时，是从你设备上的共享容器中读取任务的，这个过程不会有任何数据离开你的 iPhone。

通知

如果你允许通知，FocusDo 会在你的设备本地安排提醒和专注计时提示，不经过任何服务器。你可以随时在"设置 › 通知 › FocusDo"中关闭。

你创建的备份

你可以把任务导出为 JSON 文件，也可以从备份文件恢复。文件保存在你选择的位置（例如"文件"或 iCloud 云盘），我们永远不会收到它。

你的选择与权利

  • 导出：随时在应用的"设置 › 备份"中导出你的数据。
  • 删除：在应用中删除任务，或直接删除应用。如需删除已同步的副本，请前往 iOS"设置 › [你的姓名] › iCloud › 管理储存空间 › FocusDo"。
  • 由于我们不持有任何关于你的个人数据，我们这边没有需要查看、更正或删除的内容。

儿童

FocusDo 适合所有年龄段使用，不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。
